About this index

Architecture borrowed from the Australian Disaster Resilience Index, hazard swapped from natural disaster to AI. Risk categories from the International AI Safety Report 2026 (Bengio et al.) and An Overview of Catastrophic AI Risks (Hendrycks, Mazeika & Woodside). Legal basis: Disaster Management Act 2003 (Qld) s4A(d), s16(1)(d); Human Rights Act 2019 (Qld) ss9, 58. See "Mapping AI risk to Disaster Risk Management" above for how the response model maps onto Queensland's existing disaster planning.
